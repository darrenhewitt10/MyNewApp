Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

What Spotlight does

Spotlight checks whether AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity recommend your business when customers ask them questions, and suggests improvements. This policy explains what information we collect to do that, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Business details. The business name, city, type, website and Google Business Profile link you enter, and the customer questions you track.
  • Check results. The answers AI assistants give to those questions, your scores and your fix list.
  • Free check requests. If you use the free check on our home page: your business name, city and email address.
  • Billing. If you subscribe, our payment processor (Stripe) handles your card details. We never see or store your full card number; we keep only a customer reference and your plan.

How we use it

  • To run your checks, show your dashboard and email your reports.
  • To read your public website when writing your fix list.
  • To manage your subscription and contact you about your account.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run Spotlight. Each receives only what it needs:

  • AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity) receive the customer questions we ask on your behalf, and the answers are analyzed to find your business. Questions are written about your business type and city, not about you personally.
  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and background jobs.
  • Netlify hosts this website and keeps a copy of free check requests.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Resend delivers our emails.

Cookies and local storage

We store your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and check history while your account is open. When you remove a business, its questions, results and history are deleted. To delete your account and everything in it, email us at the address below.

Your choices

  • Turn report emails off any time in your dashboard settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it.

Do people really use AI to find local businesses?+

Yes, and more every month. Instead of scrolling search results, people ask an assistant a plain question and get two or three names back. Those names get the call.

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. We add assistants as they become popular with consumers.

Can you guarantee I’ll be recommended?+

Nobody can honestly guarantee that — the AI companies decide what their assistants say. What we can do is show you exactly where you stand and fix the signals those assistants rely on, so your odds go up week over week.

Do I need to be technical?+

Not at all. Every fix is written in plain English, like “add your Saturday hours to Google” or “ask five recent customers for a review.”

AI answers change constantly as models update and competitors improve. A one-time check goes stale fast; weekly tracking shows what’s working.
